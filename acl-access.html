Alexandria Cardiology League

Alexandria Cardiology League is a learning platform built to make cardiology education more practical, interactive and competitive. It brings together structured learning, question practice, progress tracking and friendly challenges in one place. ACL is also a first step toward a broader medical learning platform that can expand into other specialties. If you would like to participate, contribute or collaborate, we’d love to hear from you.
